That's right, I built a website. A whole lot of blood, tape and tears later and I present my corner of the internet. So let me give you the tour...

It began as any other day: wake up; put the kettle on until finally I reached a remarkably stupid apipheny. "Oh right" I recalled, I should probably make a website. Now many an indie creative has had this same though; whether it be the creative heights of [Welcome Home](https://welcomehomerestorationproject.net/) to just a simple 'I need to advertise my business' endeavour. Now I'm fluent in web code (part of my mysterious backstory) so the fact I had not made the connection was very funny - because I could have just built one at any point.

So I present... the conglomeration of all my weirdness. I feel this is a great way to have complete creative control over my work and marketing; but will get more into that later.
