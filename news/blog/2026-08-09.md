BadIdeaInc. Then BadIdeaGames. Truly I have been changing names like hairstyles. Yet this may be my biggest transformation yet; so let's discuss why my caterpillar cocooned and became a "Dont Play!" butterfly.

In fairness it was a thought that had wormed it's way into my empty head a while ago. I love the name "BadIdea" but apparently so does everyone else! It felt like every corner I turned there was someone slightly further up the food chain with some iteration of BadIdeaCompany. The breaking straw was a little known project, the [bad ideas game jam](https://itch.io/jam/bad-ideas-game-jam-2026). Now I wish I was not incredibly busy during the submission period, because I imagine it would have caused a comedic commotion if I strutted in cowboy boots and all. However it did signify the truth I had felt for a while; I was burying myself algorithmically.

"Boring", I can hear you saying. You changed your name to better suit the whims of the eldritch overlord we call the social media algorithm. Fine, my dear reader, perhaps I shall indulge you and go into the psychology behind my naming convention.

### How I Got My Usernames

My goal with my username was to create a double entendre (duel meaning). My name must reflect:
1. A horror trope/creature
2. Be incredibly dumb when read literally

So breaking down 'BadIdeaGames':
1. It's a bad idea to, for example, follow a monster into the woods
2. All our ideas are terrible

But of course, with that name basically taken, I asked myself a very simple question. **What is the stupidest thing you could name a game studio?** It was many a day I pondered this - and many a sleepless night. But then it hit me:

### Dont Play! Games

As most good idea start, it was a throwaway thought. Elaborating further though, it checked off both of my requirements perfectly:
1. The 'don't play the haunted tape I left for you' cliché
2. Don't play my games
